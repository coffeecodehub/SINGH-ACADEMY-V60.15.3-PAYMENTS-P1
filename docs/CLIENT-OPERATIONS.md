# Singh Academy — Client Admin operating guide

## Your workspace

Use the Academy Admin login supplied to you. Student login is separate. The workspace includes Courses, Team, Events, Certificates, Completion notifications and Account security. It is not a revenue or accounting dashboard.

## Courses, Team and Events

Use the existing editors to add or update course details, named modules and lessons, team profiles and events. Save images after uploading. New draft lessons must be published separately. Do not delete courses with purchase/learning history; unpublish them when appropriate. The public website retains its current design.

## Review completed learning

When a learner finishes the required course lessons, a notification appears. Open Certificates and choose Review answers. Read the saved quiz responses, assignment text and any uploaded work. Previous attempts and feedback remain available. Required evidence gaps are identified rather than replaced with assumed answers.

To approve, confirm the displayed student/course spelling, tick the review confirmation, enter an approval note and your current password, then choose Generate & issue certificate. The learner can download the issued PDF from their account.

To request improvement, enter clear feedback and your current password, confirm the review, and select Try Again — restart course. The learner starts that course from the first lesson with a new attempt. Their earlier submitted work is retained; purchases and access dates do not change. If their access has expired they will need to renew it before continuing.

Certificates are issued by your review decision, not automatically by a quiz score. Do not approve a legacy course completion as proof of answers that were never saved. Issued certificates can be revoked with a reason, but a PDF already downloaded cannot be recalled; verification will show its status.

## Student billing

Students use My Billing to see their own plan terms, invoices, payment records and expiry dates. A circle shows remaining subscription days; Re-subscribe opens the payment checkout. There is no automatic monthly charge in this version.

Keep your login and authenticator recovery codes private. Sign out after use. Tell the implementation owner about errors without sending passwords, payment secrets or private student files into public chats.
