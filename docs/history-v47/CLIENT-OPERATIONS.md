# Singh Academy — Business Admin operating guide

## What this workspace does

Use the Business Admin Panel to manage academy customers, enrollment requests, subscriptions, due dates, invoices, recorded receipts/refunds, course access, reports, website messages and existing courses/team/events/plans/reviews. Administrative access is separate from student login. Complete your account's two-step verification and save recovery codes privately.

This version manages **payments that the academy has actually received or returned**. It does not charge a card, transfer money, automatically renew a card subscription or issue a bank/card refund. Creating a record is not proof that a transfer occurred.

## From inquiry to course access

A student submits a course or plan enrollment request. Open Enrollment requests, review the student and selected item, and create an invoice with the agreed amount, currency, payment due date and access term. The student can review their own billing history.

After you independently confirm receipt of money, record the actual amount, received date, payment method and reference against the invoice. Partial receipts reduce the remaining balance. Full settlement activates the invoiced course or membership access. Do not record a receipt before money is received simply to unlock a course; use an authorized complimentary-access operation instead.

## Subscription dates and follow-up

Subscription start is when access begins. Expiry is when that access term ends. Payment due date is the deadline on an invoice. These are separate dates: an approaching expiry does not itself prove the student owes another payment.

Use expiring-membership views for renewal follow-up, then issue a renewal invoice for another paid term. An audited access-date correction or complimentary extension is not a receipt and must not appear as revenue. A renewal flag does not automatically debit a customer.

## Receipts, refunds and reports

Record a refund only after the academy has returned the money. The system keeps the original receipt history and records a matching refund/credit. Choose access revocation explicitly when appropriate; a partial refund should not silently remove all unrelated access.

Gross collections are recorded receipts. Net collections are gross collections minus recorded refunds; they are **not net profit**, because expenses, taxes and provider fees are not accounted for here. Keep different currencies separate. Validate unfamiliar historical records before relying on totals. Missing old dates or login information are not reconstructed by the new dashboard.

Use invoice balances/statuses to follow up on unpaid, partial or overdue accounts. Keep exported CSV reports private; they contain personal and financial information. Sensitive changes require password confirmation and a reason, creating a traceable activity history.

## Customers, website messages and content

Customers shows contact details, account status, signup/login information and linked billing/access history. Blocking an account prevents access without deleting its financial history. Internal notes should contain only information appropriate for business administration.

Website messages is the contact-form inbox. Mark a message Responded only after actually replying through the agreed communication channel. Changing its status does not send an email on your behalf.

Courses retain named modules and lessons. Save the course basics, add curriculum, save lesson content, then publish the lesson and course separately. Draft content is not a published course. Team profiles, events, academy plans and reviews remain manageable. Reviews require moderation before public display.

Use Unpublish rather than deleting a product with purchase history. Removing a media reference hides/detaches it from the saved content; physical storage may be retained for safety and maintenance. Ask the support team to handle storage review rather than deleting database files directly.

## Security and handover

Never share account passwords, authenticator setup keys or recovery codes in screenshots or messages. Sign out on shared devices. Report unexpected sessions or inaccurate balances before making further sensitive changes. The support team must finish staging tests, real email delivery checks and backup/restore verification before the live handover.

Automatic online billing, formal tax/profit accounting, bank reconciliation, automated reminder schedules, grading and certificates are not promised by this Business Admin release.
