1. Open your actual Vimeo and YouTube lessons, including unlisted Vimeo hash links. Each newly opened recorded lesson must begin at zero. Check poster Play, native provider Play, Pause, Seek, Fullscreen and lesson switching on desktop and a real phone. Check slow-provider response and manual Retry; do not require silent autoplay on browsers that block it.
2. Click Play immediately while the poster is still preparing. The same iframe remains mounted; early play intent can be resent once after the ready handshake, not an endless play/reload loop. Do not seek Vimeo during preparation: its documented seek-before-first-play can start playback.
3. Check course/team covers on first cold load AND after visiting Home/About then navigating via Courses/Team. First visible photos have high priority; speculative prefetch is limited to two concurrent photos, seven initial candidates and skips hidden/offline/save-data contexts. 6. At desktop widths >=1600 CSS pixels, header height is 80px (previously72), brand font29px (previously27), navigation13px (previously12), logo52x64 (previously48x58). Tablet/mobile rules and all other UI styles remain unchanged. Check long account names and browser zoom.
